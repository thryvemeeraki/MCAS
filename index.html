Academic Inspiration

Dedicated to Prof. Aditya Mukherjee & Prof. Mridula Mukherjee

Honoring the enduring scholarly legacy of two eminent JNU historians whose rigorous intellectual traditions inspire our research framework.

Prof. Aditya Mukherjee

Historian of Political Economy & Modern India

Former Dean of the School of Social Sciences and Chairperson of the Centre for Historical Studies at JNU, alongside serving as General President of the Indian History Congress. His seminal contributions to understanding Indian capitalism, imperial structures, and contemporary political economy form the structural anchor of MCAS's socio-technological framework.

Prof. Mridula Mukherjee

Historian of Agrarian Economies & Social Movements

Former Professor of Modern Indian History, Dean of the School of Social Sciences at JNU, and Director of the Nehru Memorial Museum & Library (NMML). Celebrated for her path-breaking studies on peasant movements and structural agrarian realities, her commitment to deep socio-historical context inspires our work in human behavior and policy.
